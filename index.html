About Us

Practical QHSE consulting for Oman businesses

Nahda QHSE Solutions journey started in 2025 under founder Mr. Ali Amur Al Balushi to elevate Oman’s safety, health, and compliance performance with practical, regulation-ready support. With over 17 years of construction safety leadership across the Middle East, he helps organizations grow sustainably through expert coaching, hands-on consultation, and strong QHSE systems.

FAQ

Common questions about our HSE services

What services does Nahda Safety offer?

We offer audits, compliance documentation, training, emergency planning, and AI-enabled risk advisory tailored for schools, hospitals, construction and oil & gas sites.

How do you support hospital safety?

We provide infection control audits, patient handling guidance, clinical area risk assessments and staff training to improve patient and staff safety.

Can you help with construction site safety?

Yes. We perform site inspections, toolbox talks, PPE selection, and permit-to-work systems for safe site operations.

Do you provide emergency & evacuation planning?

We develop evacuation plans, run drills, and prepare emergency response procedures aligned with Omani regulations.

How do your AI safety insights work?

Our AI tools analyse incident trends, compliance gaps and training needs to generate prioritised recommendations and action plans.

How can I get a quotation?

Contact us via WhatsApp or email with your site details and we will provide a tailored proposal and timeline.
